Peer Exchange — Check Before You Use

Exchange one checked AI note with a buddy. Review each other's marking, give feedback, and revise — then swap and do it again.

You will need a buddy and one checked note between you. The whole activity takes about 15 minutes.

Swift AI Academy · PC 4.1–4.5 · PC 5.1–5.4 · English

How this works

  • 1Exchange one checked note with your buddy — the AI version and your corrected version, side by side.
  • 2Review each other's marking against a short rubric. Give one strength, one risk, one suggestion.
  • 3Revise your note, then swap roles and run it again.
