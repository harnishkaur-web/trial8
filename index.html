Peer Exchange — Check Before You Use

Exchange one checked AI note with a buddy. Review each other's marking, give feedback, and revise — then swap and do it again.

How this works

  • 1Exchange one checked note with your buddy — the AI version and your corrected version, side by side.
  • 2Review each other's marking against a short rubric. Give one strength, one risk, one suggestion.
  • 3Revise your note, then swap roles and run it again.

You will need a buddy and one checked note between you. The whole activity takes about 15 minutes.

Step 1

Who goes first?

Decide with your buddy who plays Owner first. You'll both play both roles before you finish.

Owner shares the note. Reviewer checks it against the rubric. You swap for round 2.

Step 2

Exchange the note

Pick the setting closer to your own — ITI or higher education.

Step 3

Review against the rubric

As Reviewer, check off what you actually see in your buddy's note.

  • Every fact, figure, date, name and source in the AI draft is marked.
  • Each correction is supported by a record, clearly qualified, or removed — not guessed.
  • The corrected version reads clearly, with no confusing leftover wording.
  • At least one genuine change happened because of this review.

Step 4

Give feedback

If you disagree, say it plainly:

Reviewer

"I see this differently because ___."

Owner

"That's fair, I'll recheck it." — or — "I kept this because ___."

Step 5

Revise, on the clock

Owner revises the note using the feedback just given. Run the timer for this phase, and the ones before it, if you haven't yet.

02:00 Revision

Owner makes at least one change based on the feedback just received.

Step 6

Swap and reflect

Now swap roles — the Reviewer becomes the Owner — and run Steps 2 to 5 again for round 2.

Your evidence

Check you have all of these, then download your answers.

  • The AI note and your corrected version, side by side.
  • One strength, one risk and one suggested change from your Reviewer.
  • The one change you made after peer review.
  • Round 2 completed, with roles swapped.
